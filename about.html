About Pure Prep

Simple meals, grown from your budget.

Pure Prep builds budget-aware meal plans for you. Answer a short questionnaire—budget, shopping cadence, calorie and macro targets, meals, and which days you want to plan—and Pure Prep puts together:

  • Meal plans with meals and snacks that fit your daily targets,
  • Grocery lists with estimated prices for your shopping window, and
  • Inventory tracking and a schedule that show what’s left and when to order next.

Who it’s for

Pure Prep is made for shoppers in the United States. Prices are shown in US dollars (USD) and are estimates based on Walmart.com. The price on Walmart.com governs if it differs. See our affiliate disclosure.

Your data

No account needed. Your plans stay in your own browser. Read the privacy policy.
