Affiliate disclosure

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Pure Prep may contain affiliate links to Walmart.com. If you click one of these links and make a qualifying purchase, Pure Prep may earn a commission. This comes at no extra cost to you—you pay the same price either way.

Commissions help keep Pure Prep free. They do not change how meal plans are built: plans and grocery lists are generated from your budget, nutrition targets, and preferences.

About prices

Grocery prices shown in Pure Prep are estimates in US dollars based on Walmart.com prices as of the time shown. Prices and availability change and can vary by location. If the price on Walmart.com differs from the price shown in Pure Prep, the price on Walmart.com governs.
