Pure Prep

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Pure Prep is a free meal-planning tool. This page explains, in plain language, what information it handles and where that information goes.

Your plan stays in your browser

Pure Prep has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you enter or build in the app—your questionnaire answers (such as budget, shopping cadence, calorie and macro targets, meals and snacks, plan days, and body weight, activity level, and weight goal if you enter them), your meal plans, grocery lists, inventory, and schedule—is saved only in your browser’s localStorage on your device. The app does not send this data to us or to any server, and we cannot see it.

Because the data lives in your browser, it does not sync between devices or browsers, and private/incognito windows may discard it when closed.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load any page, GitHub receives standard web request information, including your IP address, and may log it for security and operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. Pure Prep itself does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of its own.

Links to Walmart.com

Pure Prep shows estimated grocery prices based on Walmart.com. Links to Walmart.com on this site may be affiliate links. If you click one, you leave Pure Prep, and Walmart and its affiliate network partner Impact may set or read cookies in your browser to track the referral and any qualifying purchase. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. See our affiliate disclosure for more.

We don’t sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Since your plan data never leaves your browser, we have nothing to sell.

Clearing your data

You can delete everything Pure Prep has saved at any time:

  • In your browser’s settings, clear site data (cookies and site data / local storage) for leyuh.github.io, or
  • Open your browser’s developer tools and remove the purePrepState entry under Local Storage.

Clearing your browser history and site data for all sites also removes it. The app will start fresh the next time you visit.

Children

Pure Prep is intended for adult shoppers in the United States and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue on GitHub (github.com/leyuh/pure-prep/issues).
