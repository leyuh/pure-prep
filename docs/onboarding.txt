Onboarding questionnaire

Interactive form that prompts the user for input beginning with question 1 and so on.

Input:

1. What is your monthly grocery budget?
2. How often do you want to get groceries? (Weekly, every other week, monthly)
3. What is your daily caloric goal?
   Help me find out:
   - Weight
   - Weight goal (lose, maintain, gain)
   - Activity level
4. What are your macro goals?
   - 33% p 42% c 25% f (recommended for cutting)
   - 28% p 42% c 30% f (recommended for maintenance)
   - 25% p 45% c 30% f (recommended for bulking)
5. How many meals per day?
   - 2 meals, 1 snack
   - 3 meals
   - 3 meals, 2 snacks
   - 4 meals
   - 5 meals
   - Custom
6. How many days per week will you follow your plan?
   - Checkbox list of days of week

Output:

Based on input from questionnaire, display:
- Day's total caloric and macronutrient targets
- Meal / snack list with each of their caloric targets and a suggested meal/snack based on the Meal Templates document. The suggested meal/snack's macro split must be very close to the value from question #4. Display the macronutrients of each ingredient in the meal/snack and the total macros for the meal.
  - Each meal/snack should have a reroll button that gives a new suggestion that adheres to the Meal & Snack Planning Procedure.
- Micronutrients breakdown of day's food and how close major micronutrients are to suggested daily intake.
  - For sodium, note that value does not include added salt/seasoning
- Using Walmart API, grocery list that accounts for all ingredients needed for the specified time frame (#2 in questionnaire) and the amount of days that the meal plan will be followed (#6 in questionnaire). Provide price for each item and total price for all groceries needed.
- Save & close button that returns user to dashboard.

Give user option to return to Dashboard.

Example-
Daily targets: 2400 cals, 160 g p, 240g c, 80g f

Meal 1: 600 cal - banana berry smoothie
Snack 1: 300 cal - almonds
Meal 2: 600 cal - ground beef bowl
Snack 2: 300 cal - cottage cheese & pineapple
Meal 3: 600 cal - pumpkin spice oatmeal
