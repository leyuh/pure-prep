Onboarding questionnaire

Interactive form that prompts the user for input beginning with question 1 and so on.

#4 - have the 2nd option selected by default and show the exact text I have here for each of the options

Input:
1. What is your monthly grocery budget?
2. How often do you prefer to order groceries? (Weekly, every other week, monthly)
3. What is your daily caloric goal?
   Help me find out: Weight, Weight goal (lose/maintain/gain), Activity level
4. What are your macronutrient targets?
   - 33 p | 42 c | 25 f (recommended for weight loss)
   - 28 p | 42 c | 30 f (recommended for weight maintenance) [default]
   - 25 p | 45 c | 30 f (recommended for weight gain)
   - Custom
5. How many meals do you like per day?
   - 2 meals, 1 snack
   - 3 meals
   - 3 meals, 2 snacks
   - 4 meals
   - 5 meals
6. How many days per week will you follow your plan?
   - Checkbox list of days of week

Output:
- Day’s total caloric and macronutrient targets
- Meal/snack list with suggested meals/snacks and their exact calories (curated totals, not slot targets). Macro split very close to #4. Show ingredient macros + meal totals. Per-slot reroll.
- Nutrition overview paragraph + micronutrient rating /5 stars
- Micros as small pie charts (% in center); expand for horizontal bars; sodium note under sodium
- Grocery list for cadence (#2) × plan days (#6) with item + total prices (Walmart estimates)
- Save & close → Dashboard

Example values are illustrative only — mix meals/snacks to hit the user’s daily targets.
