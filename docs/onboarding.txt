Onboarding questionnaire

Interactive form that prompts the user for input beginning with question 1 and so on.

#4 - 2nd option default; exact macro labels as written.

Input: budget, grocery cadence, calories (+ help), macros (loss/maintenance/gain/Custom), meals/day (no Custom), weekday checkboxes.

Output:
- Day’s total caloric and macronutrient targets AND actuals. Targets on the left, actual on the right. Each: calories on the left + pie chart macro distribution on the right.
- Meal/snack list from Meal Templates; macros close to #4; ingredient macros + meal totals; per-slot reroll. Show exact curated calories.
- Nutrition overview: short paragraph (3–5 sentences) on best nutritional aspects. No star rating.
- Micronutrients: collapsed expandable tab until clicked. Inside: small pie charts for vitamins/minerals with % in center; hover swaps to amount (g/mg). Sodium note under sodium.
- Grocery list for cadence × plan days with prices. Save & close → Dashboard.
