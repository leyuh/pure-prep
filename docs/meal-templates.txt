Meal Templates (synced from Drive)

- Day cal ±100 of target; macros ±2% of target split
- Snacks ~ half a meal; meals give-or-take 150 cal; snacks give-or-take 75 cal
- Produce diversity across the day
- Budget sacrifice order (sacrifice first → last):
  1. Do not require organic items
  2. Reduce overall ingredient count and variety in meals and micronutrients (if that lowers the price)
  3. Cheaper items should be used (ex: bananas and raisins over fresh berries, ground beef over salmon) where possible
- Always include weight in grams; condense units (e.g. 2 tbsp instead of 6 tsp)
- Cup items as standard fractions (⅛, ¼, ⅓, ½) or whole + fraction
- Tablespoons and teaspoons should never have decimals — opt for 4 tsp over 1.3 tbsp
- Do not repeat ingredients in the same meal (merge tsp/tbsp of same food)
- Meal 1 default: smoothie or oatmeal on initial plan
- Oatmeal milk: 4 oz per each ½ cup oats (omit if daily cals would exceed goal by >100)
- Bowl carbs: white rice, brown rice, potatoes, sweet potatoes, or quinoa (prefer cheap rice on low budget)
- Bowl vegetables: broccoli, bell peppers, cauliflower, carrots, mixed vegetables, stir fry, asparagus, or zucchini
- Salad jar protein: lean ground beef, turkey, chicken breast, chicken thighs, salmon, cod, shrimp (+ optional ¼–½ beans). No eggs/egg whites (bowls may still use eggs)
- Salad jar greens: 2–3 cups lettuce + optional spinach / kale / mixed greens; 1–3 veg portions; optional rice; fat: EVOO / avocado / hard boiled egg / feta / parmesan
- Bowl protein may include eggs/egg whites; free meal types on per-slot reroll
