Meal Templates (synced)

- Day cal ±100; macros ±2%
- Snacks ~ half a meal; meals ±150; snacks ±75
- Produce diversity; budget sacrifice order
- The meal plan should cost no more than the budget allows: the lower the budget is, the more sacrifices should be made (order = sacrifice first):
  1. Do not require organic items
  2. Reduce overall ingredient count and variety in meals and micronutrients (if that lowers the price)
  3. Cheaper items should be used (ex: bananas and raisins over fresh berries, ground beef over salmon) where possible.
- Grams + cup fractions; condense tsp→tbsp
- Do not repeat ingredients in the same meal (merge tsp/tbsp of same food)
- Oatmeal milk: 4oz per each ½ cup oats
- Salad jar greens: 2–3 cups lettuce + optional other greens
